EProlo Inventory Sync

Stock sync

Runs automatically at 03:17 and 15:17 UTC. Copies EPROLO private stock to China Warehouse for products tagged EPSync.

Cost sync

Runs automatically at 04:47 UTC. Sets Cost per item and USD cost for products tagged StockRep (not EPSync) from the latest EPROLO order, or else the EPROLO product list.

Inventory report

Created automatically every other day at 05:17 UTC: an Excel file with one tab per location, only SKUs with stock (more than 0 units).
